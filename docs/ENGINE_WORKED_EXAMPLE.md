## Assumptions in force
- Drinking water: 1 L per resident per day; weight 0.4.
- Cooling space: 0.1 persons per vulnerable resident per day; weight 0.3.
- Heat-health access: 0.005 consults per vulnerable resident per day; weight 0.15.
- Volunteer check-ins: 0.2 check-ins per vulnerable resident per day; weight 0.15.
- Stale listings (unverified for more than 14 days) count for 25% of capacity.
- Recency: full weight for 6 h, then halves every 24 h (floor 0.6) while valid; after expiry 0.4 residual, halving every 6 h, ignored after 48 h.
- Critical sites need usable support within 750 m.

## Limitations
- Decision support for triage only. Not a weather forecast, health-risk prediction or validated measure of harm.
- Service standards and weights are illustrative assumptions until replaced with curated standards.
- Coverage depends on how complete and current the resource listings are.
- Recommendations need coordinator or admin approval. Nothing is dispatched automatically.
- Each recommendation’s modelled effect is estimated on its own; effects are not additive.
- Demo records describe fictional localities and are not assessments of real places.
