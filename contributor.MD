# Contributors

Thank you to everyone who contributed to **Climate Saarthi** — a climate-response intelligence platform focused on helping identify where climate-related help and resources are missing.

Climate Saarthi is built using **MERN and JavaScript**, with React + Vite on the frontend and Node.js + Express + MongoDB + Mongoose on the backend. The project includes a Response Intelligence Engine, authentication and role-based access, a staff/admin console, and a research-driven approach to climate response.

## 🌍 About Climate Saarthi

> **"Alerts tell you where it is hot. Saarthi shows where help is missing."**

Climate Saarthi is designed as **Climate response intelligence for India**, with the broader goal of helping understand climate-related risk and identify gaps in available response resources.

The platform is intended to move beyond simply displaying climate alerts by connecting climate information with response capacity, local resources and actionable intelligence.

The project currently has a working UI shell, Response Intelligence Engine and authentication system, while several data-driven modules remain part of the planned development phases.

* India Meteorological Department (IMD)

* Central Water Commission (CWC)
* National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA)
* Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB)
* Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)
* Central Ground Water Board (CGWB)
* India-WRIS

These sources are documented in the project's README as reference sources rather than live feeds.

## 📌 Contribution Philosophy

We believe effective climate technology requires more than software alone.

It requires:

* Reliable research
* Responsible use of data
* Clear communication
* Secure engineering
* Accessible interfaces
* Collaboration across technical and non-technical roles

Climate Saarthi aims to bring these disciplines together to create technology that can support better climate-response decision making.
